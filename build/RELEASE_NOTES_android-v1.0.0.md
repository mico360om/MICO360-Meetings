## MICO360 Meetings for Android 1.0.0

The first Android version of MICO360 Meetings.

**Install:** download `MICO360Meetings-Android-1.0.0.apk` on the phone and open it (Android 8.0 or newer). Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

### What it does
- **Record** a meeting with the phone's microphone — it keeps recording with the screen off, pauses for phone calls, and warns if the microphone is sending only silence. Or **import** an audio/video file, or **paste** a transcript.
- **Transcribe on the phone** with whisper.cpp — the audio never leaves the phone. Download a speech model once (Tiny 32 MB, Base 60 MB, Small 190 MB); after that it works offline. English, Arabic or automatic; optional speaker identification with your own names.
- **Minutes from MICO360 Cloud** using the same prompts, styles and meeting types as the desktop app — long meetings are handled in parts.
- **Edit** the minutes with a Markdown toolbar and a live preview (right-to-left for Arabic).
- **Export and share** a PDF in your company's design (Classic, Modern, Formal, Compact — with letterhead, logo, footer and page numbers), or Word, text and Markdown, straight to WhatsApp, e-mail or Drive.
- **Action items** across meetings: edit, filter, CSV and calendar export, and reminders the day before and on the day.
- **Insights**: meetings per week, open and overdue items, completion rate, recurring topics.
- **Prompts and companies** import and export in the desktop app's file formats.
- **Updates**: Settings → Updates finds new Android releases and checks each download's SHA256 before installing.

### Privacy
Recordings, transcripts, minutes and settings stay on the phone. Only the transcript (with the meeting's title, date and attendees) is sent to MICO360 Cloud to write the minutes; while that connection is unencrypted the app asks first.
